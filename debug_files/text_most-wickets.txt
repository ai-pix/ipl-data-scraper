The 2025 Indian Premier League is the 18th edition of the two-month-long cricketing extravaganza. IPL 2025 starts off on March 22 with a clash between Kolkata Knight Riders and Royal Challengers Bengaluru at the Eden Gardens. The iconic venue in Kolkata will also see the end of IPL 2025 since the final will be held there on May 25.
Ahead of the 2025 season, Rajasthan Royals spinner Yuzvendra Chahal leads the list for the most wicket takers with 205 scalps. In his IPL career, Chahal has taken one five-wicket haul and six four-wicket hauls. He is followed by Piyush Chawla on the charts with 192 wickets from 192 innings. Retired CSK and West Indian legend Dwayne Bravo is third with 183 wickets and Mumbai Indians’ Piyush Chawla who comes third with 179 wickets.
Bhuvneshwar Kumar (181 wickets) and Sunil Narine (180) round up the top 5 after 17 seasons of IPL.
In the 2024 season, Punjab Kings’ Harshal Patel with 24 wickets from 14 innings edged out KKR’s Varun Chakaravarthy (21 wickets) and Mumbai’s Jasprit Bumrah (20) to claim the Purple Cap awarded to the player with the most wickets in a season.
